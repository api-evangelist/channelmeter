# ChannelMeter

> ChannelMeter is a creator management and payments platform for multi-channel networks, agencies, and brands. Founded in 2012 (San Francisco), it handles creator discovery, onboarding, contracts, analytics, and automated global payouts. Creator Pay moves money to creators across 21+ currencies and 50+ countries; Creator Cash offers cash advances. It reports $1B+ paid to 1M+ creators, using Stripe and Fifth Third Bank for financial services. Backed by 500 Global.
